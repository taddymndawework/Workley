Why Workley exists

Where student skills
meet real opportunity.

Workley helps South Africa's university ICT students get seen, get hired and earn - while giving businesses fast, affordable access to fresh talent.

The problem we're solving

Talented students, hidden in plain sight

South Africa has thousands of bright, hardworking university ICT students. The problem isn't a lack of skill - it's a lack of exposure. Most never get a real chance to show employers what they can do, so their talent goes unseen and they stay underemployed.

For underprivileged students it's even harder. Money is tight, the right connections aren't there, and staying in university becomes a struggle - even when the ability clearly is. Workley exists to change that by giving every student a free stage to prove themselves.

Show what you can do, on a fair playing field - and earn while you're at it.

Make real money during your studies or between applications - income that helps you stay in school. And as clients discover and hire you, you build the experience that leads to formal work.
