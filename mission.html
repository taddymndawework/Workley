Our mission

Unlocking the potential ofSouth Africa's young talent.

We believe a student's skill - not their connections - should open the door to real opportunity.

Workley exists to bridge the gap between ambitious South African students and the clients who need their skills - fairly, safely, and with dignity for everyone involved.

A message from our founder

Why I built Workley

I grew up surrounded by brilliant, hard-working young people whose talent was never the problem - access was. Skilled students and graduates were everywhere, yet the opportunities to prove themselves were locked behind networks they didn't have.

Workley is my answer to that. A place where a verified profile and real skill are enough to be seen. Where a student in Polokwane has the same shot as one in Sandton. Where clients can hire emerging talent with confidence, and where young people earn not just an income, but a reputation they own.

This is only the beginning. Every profile created, every project completed, and every student onboarded is a step toward a South Africa where potential is never wasted for lack of a chance.

Thank you for being part of it.

Taddy Mndawe
